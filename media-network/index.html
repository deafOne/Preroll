HOW IT WORKS

Built for recurring partnerships.

Submit one inquiry. Preroll reviews the business, proposed creative and placement fit, then confirms availability and terms. Approved campaigns run for the agreed period and are reviewed against the agreed reporting plan.

No guaranteed impressions, traffic volume, leads or sales are represented here. Live counters above are current site-data records, not audience or advertising reach metrics.

Initial partnerships are handled by proposal and manual approval. Automated checkout, subscription billing and self-serve campaign management are not enabled yet.

Who can inquire?

Licensed cannabis businesses and lawful ancillary providers whose services meet applicable advertising rules.

How is pricing set?

Pricing depends on placement, duration, creative requirements, exclusivity and verified audience data.

Is editorial coverage for sale?

No. Paid placements are labeled. Sponsorship does not guarantee news coverage, rankings or favorable reviews.

Are campaigns reviewed?

Yes. Advertiser eligibility, audience fit, creative, age controls and applicable New York requirements are reviewed before launch.
