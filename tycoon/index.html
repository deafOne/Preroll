Infinity Cannabis Tycoon is a fictional business and cultivation game. It is for adults 21 and older. No real-world sales or transactions occur.

How to play

Explore: WASD or the mobile joystick. Drag the screen to orbit the camera. Space to jump, Shift to run.

Interact: Walk near a building and press E. At the greenhouse, buy seeds and plant them in empty beds; return after the grow timer and harvest. Sell harvest at the dispensary.

Drive: Press F or DRIVE near the green pickup. WASD controls the vehicle. Press F again to exit.

Progress: Use the warehouse for supplies and headquarters for upgrades. Your progress saves locally on this device.
