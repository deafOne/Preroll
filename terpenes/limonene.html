Citrus aroma and cannabis chemistry

Limonene

Limonene is a terpene found in cannabis and many other plants. It is commonly associated with citrus-like aroma. A terpene listing describes chemistry and aroma; it does not guarantee a particular effect.

What this page tells you

Commonly listed alongside other cannabis terpenes; concentrations vary by cultivar and batch.

Profile: Citrus

Cannabis chemistry varies by cultivar, producer, harvest and batch. Product labels and laboratory results, when available, are the appropriate source for current potency and terpene data.
