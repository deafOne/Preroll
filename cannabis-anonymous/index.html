WHY THIS EXISTS

The stigma didn't disappear
when the laws changed.

For years, cannabis culture was flattened into stereotypes. People hid parts of themselves, families stayed quiet, and ordinary stories were treated like something to be ashamed of. This wall makes room for those stories — the funny, difficult, complicated, proud, and unfinished ones.
